# Demo video attribution

The bundled `assets/demo_road.webm` is the 480p WebM transcode of **Garbsen 2019.webm**, a 71-second recording of a truck-safety pilot at a road junction in Garbsen, Germany. The scene was recorded by C.Suthorn and shows real road traffic associated with the Bike-Flash cyclist warning demonstration. This is demonstration footage, not a labeled evaluation clip and not proof of calibrated truck blind zones.

The project uses the 480p Wikimedia transcode without edits to the scene.
